About Me

A little about
who I am

I'm Aaryav, a Grade 11 student at Kothari International School, Noida, with a deep interest in business, entrepreneurship, and the way global markets move. From captaining my school basketball team to moderating MUN committees, I've always gravitated towards roles where I can lead, think on my feet, and bring people together around a shared goal.

Academically, I scored 97.2% in my Grade 10 CBSE boards — including a perfect 100 in Social Studies and Spanish, 99 in AI, and 95 in English. I also speak Spanish at a B1 level, which reflects my genuine interest in global communication and culture. This followed a 95% in Grade 9, and I intend to carry that momentum forward.

Outside the classroom, I've dipped my toes into the world of startups — from an early experiment with dropshipping to currently working on a nutraceutical supplement brand. I'm looking to pursue Business Administration or International Business abroad, targeting universities in Singapore, Australia, and Hong Kong where business meets a truly international perspective.
